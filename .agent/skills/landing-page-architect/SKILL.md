# Landing Page Architect

The architecture consists of 8 specific sections designed to guide the user from initial interest to conversion. When designing or reviewing a landing page, you should evaluate if it follows this structure and suggest improvements based on these principles.

## Usage Guidelines

When asked to design or review a landing page:
1. **Analyze the current state:** Does the page follow the 8-step architecture?
2. **Identify gaps:** Which sections are missing or poorly executed?
3. **Draft the content:** Work sequentially from the Hero section down to the Footer. Make sure each section fulfills its specific "Job".
4. **Iterate:** Ensure the copy is punchy, problem-focused, and value-driven.

## 8-Step Landing Page Architecture

### 1. HERO
**Structure:** Eyebrow -> Headline -> Subheadline -> CTA -> Trust Signals
**Job:** Get email or get scroll (capture attention immediately).

### 2. SUCCESS (conditional)
**Structure:** Checkmark -> Confirmation -> Deliverable list
**Job:** Kill buyer's remorse (reassure them immediately after taking action).

### 3. PROBLEM-AGITATE
**Structure:** 3 problems with agitation -> Personal transition
**Job:** Make status quo painful (show you understand their pain points).

### 4. VALUE STACK
**Structure:** 4 tiers descending -> Total value -> Your price
**Job:** Make saying no feel stupid (demonstrate overwhelming value compared to cost).

### 5. SOCIAL PROOF
**Structure:** Header -> 3 testimonials with specific results
**Job:** Let others convince them (build trust through real customer success).

### 6. TRANSFORMATION
**Structure:** 4 stages: Quick win -> Compound -> Advantage -> 10x
**Job:** Make outcome tangible (paint a picture of the positive future state).

### 7. SECONDARY CTA
**Structure:** Avatar stack -> Question headline -> "Yes" button
**Job:** Catch the scrollers (give them another chance to convert at the bottom).

### 8. FOOTER
**Structure:** Logo -> Nav -> Legal -> Social
**Job:** Professional legitimacy (provide necessary links and build credibility).
